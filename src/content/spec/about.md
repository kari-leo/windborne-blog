# About

**WindBorne** is my notebook on robotics, constellations of small machines, and the
quiet space in between — control loops, perception, embedded systems, and whatever
side detours catch my attention along the way.

I write here mostly to think out loud: half-finished ideas, things I had to learn
the hard way, and the occasional walkthrough I wish had existed when I was stuck.

The site itself is built with [Astro](https://astro.build) on a heavily customized
fork of the [Fuwari](https://github.com/saicaca/fuwari) theme. The source lives at
[github.com/kari-leo/windborne-blog](https://github.com/kari-leo/windborne-blog).

If something here is wrong, or you just want to talk shop, the GitHub link in the
sidebar is the best place to find me.`
